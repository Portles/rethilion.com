1. What the App Provides

Punch Strength is an Apple Watch fitness tool that detects punch-like wrist movements, calculates relative movement scores, and displays workout statistics and local session history. With the necessary permissions, it also saves boxing workouts to Apple Health and displays available heart-rate and active-energy data. Demo mode uses sample data and does not represent your physical performance.

2. Measurement Limits

Scores are calculated from acceleration and rotation measured at the wrist. They have no fixed maximum, but a higher score does not establish a particular impact force, punching ability, or level of fitness. The App does not measure force in newtons, impact energy, or fist velocity. It cannot verify contact with a target or consistently distinguish every punch from other similar arm movements.

Only the arm wearing your Watch is measured. Results can vary with Watch hardware, sensor availability, sampling, fit and position, technique, calibration, and detection sensitivity. Movements may be missed or counted incorrectly. Comparisons are most meaningful with consistent placement and settings; they are not certified rankings or laboratory measurements. Heart rate and energy figures depend on Apple Health and device availability and may be delayed, estimated, or unavailable.

3. Exercise Safety and Health

The App provides general fitness information. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service, personal trainer, or substitute for professional medical advice. Do not use it to diagnose or treat a condition, decide whether exercise is medically safe for you, or make emergency decisions.

Exercise within your abilities and use a safe training space, appropriate technique, and suitable protective equipment. Keep your Watch securely fitted and follow the device manufacturer's guidance. Do not strike hard surfaces, people, or unsafe objects, or increase impact or exertion merely to obtain a higher score. Avoid looking at or operating the screen during movements that require your full attention. Stop exercising if you experience pain, dizziness, or other concerning symptoms and seek appropriate medical help. Obtain qualified guidance when you are unsure whether a workout is suitable for you.
