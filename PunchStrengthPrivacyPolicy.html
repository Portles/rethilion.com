Punch Strength Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 18, 2026

Terms of Use

Punch Strength (the "App") is provided by Nizamet Özkan ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains the information the App processes, where it is stored, and the choices available to you. It also covers information you voluntarily send us for support and visits to this policy website.

1. Overview

Punch Strength processes workout information on your Apple Watch. We do not operate a server that receives your motion readings, punch scores, workout history, heart rate, or active energy. The App does not require an account and includes no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

2. Motion Data and Local Workout History

When you start a real session, the App uses Core Motion to read wrist acceleration, rotation rate, and sensor timestamps, including during calibration. It processes these readings on your Watch to detect punch-like movements and calculate a relative movement score. The continuous raw sensor stream is not saved in your history.

The App saves the following information locally so you can review completed sessions:

  • Session identifiers, start and end times, and active workout duration.
  • Detected-punch identifiers and timestamps, movement scores, peak acceleration, peak rotation rate, and movement duration.
  • Detection sensitivity and haptic-feedback preferences.

Punch counts, averages, best scores, tempo, and other statistics are calculated from these records. Local session and punch identifiers organize your records; they are not sent to us as tracking identifiers. Demo mode uses sample data and does not save real workout history or Apple Health workouts.

3. Apple Health and HealthKit

Real workout sessions use HealthKit. The App requests permission to write workouts and active energy and to read heart rate and active energy. Workout-writing permission is required to start a real session. Heart-rate and active-energy readings are optional; if access or readings are unavailable, those metrics remain unavailable. Demo mode can be explored without HealthKit access.

With the necessary permissions, the App displays heart rate and active energy during your workout and saves completed boxing workouts, together with available workout data, to Apple Health through HealthKit. Cancelled workouts are discarded. Punch scores and detailed punch records are not written to HealthKit, and heart rate and active energy are not included in the App's separate local session-history file.

Health and motion information is used only to provide your workout features. We do not use or disclose it for advertising, marketing, profiling, data brokerage, or research. We do not upload it to our servers. Apple controls Health data storage and any synchronization or backup you enable through its services. See Apple's Health App & Privacy information.

4. Storage and Retention

Workout history and preferences are stored in the App's local storage on your Watch. The normal history retains up to the most recent 200 completed real sessions; older entries are removed from the active history when a new history file is successfully saved. If a history file cannot be read because it is damaged, the App may preserve a local recovery copy to avoid losing your records. Recovery copies do not expire automatically and remain until the App's local data is removed.

The App does not provide its own cloud account, cloud backup, or cloud synchronization service. Apple-managed device backups or Health synchronization may retain or restore information according to your settings and Apple's policies. Records saved to Apple Health have a separate retention lifecycle from the App's local history.

5. Your Choices and Deletion

  • You can stop motion processing by ending or cancelling your session.
  • You can manage or revoke the App's Health access using Apple's Health and privacy controls. Motion access can be managed where your operating system provides that control. Changing permissions may limit real-workout features.
  • The current App has no in-app history-deletion or export control. Deleting the App and its associated local data from your Watch removes local history, preferences, and recovery copies, subject to any separate device backups you retain.
  • Deleting the App or revoking permissions does not automatically delete workouts already saved to Apple Health. Use Apple's Health data-management controls to review or delete those records separately.
  • Manage any device backups separately through Apple's controls. Restoring a backup may restore data that was previously removed from the device.

We cannot view, export, correct, or delete your on-device workout records remotely because we do not hold a copy. For information you have sent us directly, you may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify a request and retain information where a legal obligation requires it. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

6. Support Correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as a message, screenshots, or diagnostic details. Please do not send health information unless it is necessary for your request. We use correspondence to answer you, troubleshoot the App, and meet related legal obligations. Our email provider processes these messages to deliver and store them.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion by email. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them. Information may be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect legal rights; this does not give us access to workout data stored only on your devices.

7. Apple Services and This Website

Apple provides the App Store, watchOS, HealthKit, and any device backup, Health synchronization, or system diagnostic services you enable. These services may process information under Apple's Privacy Policy. The App does not implement an automatic diagnostic upload to us. We may receive reports or information you choose to share through Apple's own support or diagnostic-sharing options.

These policy pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies added by us. Website hosting is separate from the App. When these pages are served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's hosting documentation and GitHub's Privacy Statement. Visiting this website does not upload your App workout history.

8. Security and International Processing

Local records rely on the storage protections provided by your device and operating system. Keep your devices and backups secure and your software up to date. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. If you contact us or use Apple or website-hosting services, correspondence or service information may be processed outside your country under the relevant provider's policies and applicable legal safeguards.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general fitness tool and is not specifically directed to children. It does not require a profile, birth date, or account. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the App. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can address the request and delete the information as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable requirements. Updates will be posted here with a revised date. Where a change requires notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide that notice or obtain consent before applying the change. Publishing an update alone does not authorize a new use of your Health or motion data.
